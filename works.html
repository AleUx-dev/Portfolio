Plantira

Plantira is a plant care helper app, aimed to help it’s users get into gardening hobby without getting overwhelmed.

The main goal of Plantira is to have clean design for easier navigation and organized information structure that makes it easier to learn about gardening and keep track of user’s plant’s needs. Plantira is supposed to be an easy-to-use app for a first time gardeners.
